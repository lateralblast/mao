    mao (Multi Audio/AirPlay Output)

    Attribution

    Lines up the AirPlay receivers. Uses the Mac's microphone, and asks first.

    Click anywhere to go back.

    Calibrate sync

    This lines up the AirPlay receivers so they play together.

    mao plays a short click track through each receiver in turn, with the others muted. Your Mac's microphone listens to the room while this happens, for about 45 seconds per receiver, to hear when each one plays. The recording is deleted once it has been measured.

    It needs at least two AirPlay receivers streaming, and no output on this Mac ticked. Keep the room quiet while it runs.
